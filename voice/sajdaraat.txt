Raat ke do baje, Arham ka phone, chupke se chamka.
Ek ladki se chhupi baatein, ghar mein kisi ko pata nahi.
Usne socha, bas baatein hi toh hain, isme kya gunaah?
Par har raat dil bhaari hota gaya, namaz chhoot-ti gayi.
Phir ek raat, usne Quran mein padha, zina ke kareeb bhi mat jao.
Kareeb bhi nahi, kyunki yeh raasta, chhoti baaton se shuru hota hai.
Arham ne phone rakha, wuzu kiya, aur sajde mein ro pada.
Agle din, usne Ammi se kaha, mujhe nikah karna hai.
Allah ke liye jo chhoda, Allah usse behtar lauta deta hai.
