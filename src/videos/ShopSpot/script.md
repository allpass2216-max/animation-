## Right answer (keep secret until Part 2)
**A, outside the RTO office.** Everyone in that line needs photocopies of their documents and passport photos *right now*,
so the need is created on the spot, every day, and they can't go elsewhere.
- B (mall) is tempting: biggest crowd, but they came to shop and eat, not to photocopy, and mall rent would eat ₹50,000 in months.
- C (next to the old xerox shop) is tempting: "proven demand", but it means splitting the same customers in a price war.
- D (own street) is the careless one: zero rent, but zero people who need a copy.
