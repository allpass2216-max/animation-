## Our version (idea + lesson only)
New city, family, numbers and wording: **Verma ji in Kanpur**, his daughter **Sneha**; 800 guests; an **₹8 lakh personal loan**
for the show. Practical twist: the EMI maths. Checked: ₹8,00,000 at 14% a year for 5 years = EMI about ₹18,600 a month,
total repaid about **₹11.2 lakh** (60 x 18,615 = 11,16,900), so "aath lakh ban gaye lagbhag gyaarah lakh".

## 1. VIDEO IDEA
Verma ji takes an ₹8 lakh loan so 800 people can praise his daughter's wedding; after one night of "wah", five years of EMIs
turn ₹8 lakh into about ₹11 lakh, and none of the guests pays a single one.

## 2. SEEKH
Celebrate only as much as your own savings allow; a loan for show costs far more than its price (interest), and society's praise
pays none of it.
