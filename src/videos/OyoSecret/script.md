## Right answer (keep secret until Part 2)
**A, tell only your own truth.** ("Main kal raat girlfriend ke saath tha, sorry, aage se bata ke jaunga.")
- Your night out is yours to confess; Chachu's job loss is **his** to tell, when he is ready.
- Owning up breaks the "you stay quiet, I stay quiet" lock without exposing anyone else.
- B (both truths) is tempting because it sounds fully honest, but it shames Chachu in front of Dadi with news that isn't yours.
- C (keep both secrets) is tempting because it's the deal, but it means lying to Papa, and Chachu can hold it over you forever.
- D (expose Chachu, hide yourself) is the careless one: you save yourself by sacrificing someone else.
Part 2 payoff idea: after dinner Chachu says "shukriya", and the next day he tells the family himself, and gets support.
