## 1. VIDEO IDEA
Two quick "fitrat" analogies, then a real-life story: Aman did everything for his friend Rohit, and on the one night Aman needed
him, Rohit's phone was off. The twist: Rohit didn't change, the signs were always there.

## 2. SEEKH
A person's real nature shows in small everyday actions, not in sweet words, so give love freely but give trust by watching actions.
